About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Tübingen, with a background in palaeogenetics, palaeoepigenetics and population genetics.

I completed my PhD at Johannes Gutenberg University of Mainz, where I invesitgated DNA methylation in ancient genomes, focusing on its applications in reconstructing biological characteristics related to subsistence differnces in past human groups and evaluating its use for estimating age-at-death.

My research interests lie in using, ancient, genomic data and computational approaches to better understand the biology and history of past human populations.

My current research focuses on the population genetic and demographic characterisation of Bronze Age human populations, in the context of past disease outbreaks.
